Part One
Code of Conduct — Rules & Standards
01
Arrival & Punctuality

All CBU coaches are required to arrive a minimum of one hour and fifteen minutes (1:15) before the first game of each day. This is a non-negotiable standard with no exceptions. Being on time is a direct reflection of your professionalism and your respect for our players, families, and program.

1st
Offense
Verbal Warning
2nd
Offense
$50 Fine
3rd
Offense
Program Removal
Note: CBU understands that circumstances such as heavy traffic or situations involving multiple late players can arise. These are acknowledged on a case-by-case basis. However, the standard exists to ensure coaches lead with accountability — it is not optional.
02
Coaching Environment
Positive Culture
Coaches are required to provide players with a positive, constructive coaching environment at all times. Our goal is to develop not only athletes, but strong individuals who will carry the lessons of this program into their adult lives.
Language Standards
The use of profanity is strictly prohibited in any interaction with players, families, or officials — on the field or in any setting where you represent CBU.
Family Inclusion
Coaches must foster an environment where players and their families feel welcomed, respected, and encouraged. Our families invest significantly in this program and deserve to feel that investment is valued.
03
Program Representation

How a coach conducts themselves is a direct reflection of their respect for the CBU program. At all times — whether on the field, in the dugout, at a tournament venue, or in public — coaches are ambassadors of this organization.

Standard of Conduct
Coaches are expected to carry themselves with professionalism, integrity, and sportsmanship in every situation. Your actions represent every player, family, and staff member who belongs to CBU.
04
Alcohol & Substance Policy

CBU takes its public image seriously. There are no warnings under this policy. Violations carry immediate financial penalties or removal from the program.

1st
Offense
$100 Fine
2nd
Offense
Immediate Program Removal
✕
Zero Tolerance — Immediate Expulsion
Any coach found visibly impaired by alcohol while wearing CBU attire in public will be immediately expelled from the program without pay. No warnings. No exceptions.
No Alcohol in CBU Gear
No alcohol or controlled substances may be consumed while wearing any CBU-branded clothing or gear in public. If you choose to consume alcohol, you must change into non-CBU attire beforehand.
While Watching Other Games
Alcohol may not be consumed while watching any game at a CBU event, even if you are not actively coaching that game.
Rain Delays & Standby Situations
No alcohol may be consumed during rain delays or any situation in which there is a possibility that you may be called upon to coach the remainder of the day.
The standard is simple: if there is any chance you may coach, or any chance you are representing CBU in public, alcohol is off the table. When in doubt, do not consume.
05
Team Integrity & Impartiality

CBU is a team — and our coaches must reflect that in every setting, on and off the field. To protect both coaches and players, and to ensure no family ever feels excluded or that favoritism exists within this program, the following standards are in effect.

No Individual Dinners or Outings
Coaches may not accept invitations to, or arrange, private dinners or individual social outings with any player or parent. This applies regardless of intent — the appearance of favoritism is just as damaging as favoritism itself.
Whole-Team Rule
Group outings, team dinners, or social events are welcome and encouraged — but only when the entire team and their families are invited. If it is not open to everyone, it cannot involve a coach.
No Favoritism — Real or Perceived
Coaches must not single out individual players or families for special treatment, preferential access, or exclusive relationships of any kind. Every player on this roster deserves equal investment and attention from their coaching staff.
Why this matters: Parents and players are watching how coaches treat each other's families. When coaches show up for everyone equally, it builds the culture of unity that makes CBU teams successful — and makes every family proud to be part of this program.
06

Part Two
Summer Coaches Operations Manual
01
Communication Responsibilities

The Head Coach is responsible for all communication with players and families. Clear, proactive communication is the foundation of a well-run CBU team.

Schedule & Pitching Rotation — 48 Hour Rule
Once event schedules are released, coaches have 48 hours to submit pitching rotations to their team and their Grad Class Director.
Required Posting Locations
All schedules and uniform information must be posted in both TeamSnap and the Team Telegram Chat. Both platforms must be kept current at all times.
02
Daily Game Preparation

Head Coaches are expected to run a structured and efficient pregame routine each day. There are no exceptions and no "show and goes."

Required Pregame Routine
  • Batting Practice (BP)
  • Infield — ground balls
  • Outfield — fly balls
Multi-game days: If playing multiple games in a day, a second infield and outfield is not required unless desired. Read the players' energy level and conditions of the day.
03
Lineup & Playing Time

Playing time should be roughly even among all positional players. Every rostered player must receive consistent and meaningful opportunities to play.

Hitter Count Guidelines
  • Pool play: 11 hitters encouraged
  • Playoffs: Adjust to 10 hitters if necessary
  • Once a lineup structure is established, maintain it consistently
No Player Sits an Entire Game
No positional player should sit an entire game. Both coaches on staff must actively monitor playing time and make mid-inning changes if necessary. Complete blowouts are understood, but awareness is expected at all times.
04
Player & Parent Communication
Player Check-In
Check on player health and availability before each event. If a player is not in the lineup, communicate directly and clearly why — rest day, pitching preparation, or rotation management. Transparency builds trust and prevents misunderstandings.
No Pre/Post Game Parent Meetings
Coaches are NOT permitted to hold meetings with parents before or after games. If a parent reaches out with a concern, instruct them to wait until the conclusion of the current event. CBU enforces a 24 to 48 hour cooling-off period before any meeting or phone call is scheduled — this allows all parties to decompress, think clearly, and approach the conversation with the right mindset. Once that window has passed, a phone call may be scheduled and coaches may request their Grad Class Director be included.
Player Accountability
CBU does not bench players based solely on performance. However, players will be removed for: lack of effort, throwing equipment, poor sportsmanship, or disrespectful behavior or body language. All players are expected to represent CBU with high energy and professionalism.
No 1-on-1 Meetings
Coaches must never hold a personal in-person meeting alone with a player or with a player and their parents. A CBU representative must always be present — either an assistant coach or a director. All meetings must take place in a public setting. This protects both the coach and the player at all times.
Purpose: This structure ensures coaches remain focused on game responsibilities and prevents emotional, in-the-moment conflicts with families.
05
Pitcher Usage & Expectations

Head Coaches are responsible for all pitching decisions — not players, parents, outside pitching coaches, or agents.

Pitcher-Only (PO) Players
Coaches are strongly encouraged to have PO players attend the entire event, unless they are high-level priority arms. Lower-rotation pitchers who only want to attend select games are not guaranteed a pitching opportunity if they are not available according to team needs.
Head Coach Authority
The Head Coach dictates when pitchers throw. If a player is not available according to team needs, they may not be guaranteed a pitching opportunity.
06
Lodging Policy

CBU provides one hotel room per team staff per event, or an Airbnb for the entire grad class depending on event setup.

Individual Room Requests
CBU does not budget for individual hotel rooms outside of assigned team lodging. If a coach wants their own room, CBU will pay 50% of the going rate of the booked room. The coach may stay wherever they choose with that contribution.
Example
If the event hotel rooms total $1,000 — CBU will send $500 to the coach requesting their own room.
07
Travel & Reimbursement
Standard Mileage Rate
$0.30 / mile
Round trip from start to destination
WWBA Rate
$0.40 / mile
Due to extensive WWBA travel
Flights
Flying to an event is entirely up to you. If you choose to fly, you will book your own tickets and the cost will come out of your travel money. CBU will not book flights on your behalf — this is your call to make based on your own preference and budget.
Rental Cars
If you need a rental car, that is also your call. You will book it yourself and the cost comes out of your travel money. We recommend booking through Enterprise and using the CBU corporate code XZ09986 for discounted rates. This code is for CBU business use only — do not share it or use it for personal travel.
Meals
CBU does not provide separate meal money — this is factored into your season pay. CBU cookouts will be held at select events as an organization.
Carpooling: If carpooling with another coach, both coaches still receive their full mileage money. It is up to the coaches to decide how to split any shared travel costs between themselves.
08
Coach Compensation

Coach pay is based on selections made in the pre-summer Google Form. Payments are processed after each event.

Payment Methods
  • ACH Transfer
  • Zelle
  • Venmo — special cases only
09
Coach Gear & Equipment

CBU will provide all summer coaches with the following gear:

2 Game Hats
Choice of Bucket Hat or Trucker Hat
5 Shirts
4 Pairs of Shorts
Travel Bag / Backpack
Manager Materials
  • Lineup Cards (box)
  • Dugout Charts
  • Metal Clipboard (if not already owned)
10
In-Game Communication System

Each team will be provided with a 3-piece radio communication system for calling pitches directly to the catcher. Radios will be returned at the end of the season.

Units Provided
Head Coach
Assistant Coach
Catcher
Return Policy
All radios returned
at end of season
Competitive advantage: This system is one of our biggest edges. Coaches are expected to use it consistently and effectively. Controlling the running game and calling pitches are where great coaches impact games — and CBU believes it has the best coaches in the industry.

Part Three
Directors Operations Manual
01
Foundation

Everything outlined in the Coaches Operations Manual and Code of Conduct applies to all directors without exception. The following standards are in addition to those existing requirements.

02
Schedule Requirements
Schedule Submission — 24 to 48 Hour Rule
Grad Class Directors must supply a schedule of games they will be attending to National Directors within 24 to 48 hours after the schedule is released. National Directors reserve the right to modify a Grad Class Director's schedule as needed.
Daily Composite Schedule
Grad Class Directors must provide a daily composite schedule of each team in an event to National Directors. This must be kept current and accurate throughout the event.
Full Event Attendance Required
Grad Class Directors must stay for the entire event until the last team is finished. If a director leaves an event early, their pay will be prorated accordingly based on the percentage of the event missed.
Minimum Game Requirement
A Grad Class Director must attend a minimum of 2 games per day where applicable. Directors must also make sure to schedule time with every team in their grad class multiple times throughout the event — not just the top team.
03
Roles & Responsibilities
National Directors
National Directors sit at the top of the CBU hierarchy and have authority to override all other directors. They receive reports from Grad Class Directors and Recruiting Directors and serve as the major decision makers for roster moves, internal and external conflict resolution, and CBU organizational growth.
Grad Class Directors
  • Organize grad class activities and communicate with coaches
  • Hold coaches accountable to CBU standards and this handbook
  • Communicate and resolve roster issues with coaches
  • Relay information and reports to National Directors
  • Generate player reports with Recruiting Coordinators (for recruitable ages)
  • Recruit external players for future CBU rosters
Recruiting Directors
  • Coordinate directly with college programs
  • Meet with families and players regarding player reports and college fits
  • Generate and maintain player reports for all recruitable players in the grad class
  • Remain available for meetings with players and families
  • Communicate with Grad Class Directors and coaches for report assistance
04
Director Standards & Consequences

If a director fails to abide by their schedule, arrives late, does not show up, fails to attend a minimum of 2 games per day, or violates any standard in the Coaches Handbook, the following consequences apply:

1st
Offense
Verbal Warning
2nd
Offense
$100 Fine
3rd
Offense
Relieved of Duties
05
Director Pay & Proration

Directors are paid per event. If a director arrives late or leaves early, pay will be docked based on the percentage of the event missed:

Event Missed Pay Reduction
10% of event missed 20% pay reduction
20% of event missed 40% pay reduction
30% of event missed 60% pay reduction
40% of event missed 80% pay reduction
50% or more of event missed No pay
